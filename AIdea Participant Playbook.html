Before you startSebelum mulai

Sign inLogin

aidea.godrejindonesia.com

  • Use your Godrej Windows account. In Work Email, type your email (you@godrejcp.com) or network ID. In Microsoft Login Password, type your Windows password.Gunakan akun Windows Godrej Anda. Di Work Email, ketik email (you@godrejcp.com) atau network ID. Di Microsoft Login Password, ketik password Windows Anda.
  • There is no separate AIdea password and nothing to register. Your account is created the first time you sign in.Tidak ada password AIdea terpisah dan tidak perlu mendaftar. Akun Anda dibuat otomatis saat pertama kali login.
  • You stay signed in while you are active, for up to 24 hours.Anda tetap login selama aktif, maksimal 24 jam.

Your menuMenu Anda

  • Overview shows your next step and the program timeline.Overview menampilkan langkah berikutnya dan jadwal program.
  • My Ideas lists your drafts and submitted ideas with every published result.My Ideas berisi draf dan ide yang sudah dikirim beserta semua hasil yang dipublikasikan.
  • Submit New Idea, Voting, Mentor Profile and Notifications.Submit New Idea, Voting, Mentor Profile, dan Notifications.
  • On a phone, the last three are under More.Di ponsel, tiga menu terakhir ada di More.

Your profileProfil Anda

  • Name, email, job title and department come from Active Directory and refresh at each sign-in. You can't edit them in AIdea.Nama, email, jabatan, dan departemen diambil dari Active Directory dan diperbarui setiap login. Data ini tidak bisa diubah di AIdea.
  • If something is wrong, or your account is locked or your password expired, contact Godrej IT.Jika ada yang salah, atau akun terkunci maupun password kedaluwarsa, hubungi Godrej IT.

Three rules that explain almost everythingTiga aturan yang menjelaskan hampir semuanya

Submitting locks your ideaSubmit mengunci ide Anda

Save drafts as often as you like. Once you click Submit idea, nothing can be edited, so check the team, text and mentors first.Simpan draf sesering yang Anda mau. Setelah klik Submit idea, tidak ada yang bisa diedit lagi, jadi periksa tim, isi, dan mentor terlebih dulu.

Results appear when they are publishedHasil muncul saat dipublikasikan

The program team publishes each stage's results for all ideas at once. Until then My Ideas shows no change, even if a decision has been made.Tim program mempublikasikan hasil setiap tahap untuk semua ide sekaligus. Sebelum itu My Ideas tidak berubah, meskipun keputusan sudah dibuat.

You see outcomes, not scoresAnda melihat hasil, bukan nilai

You see Pass / Not pass and Build / Not build. Mentor reviews, scores and judges' comments stay with the program team.Anda melihat Pass / Not pass dan Build / Not build. Review mentor, nilai, dan komentar juri hanya untuk tim program.

  1. 1

    Prepare and submit your ideaSiapkan dan kirim ide Anda

    YouAnda

    Where:Menu: Submit New Idea · My Ideas (to continue a draftuntuk melanjutkan draf)

    Your stepsLangkah Anda

    1. Agree your team first. Teammates must have signed in to AIdea at least once, or the search can't find them.Sepakati tim terlebih dulu. Anggota tim harus sudah pernah login ke AIdea, jika belum mereka tidak akan muncul di pencarian.
    2. Look through Mentor Profile and pick two mentors whose expertise fits your idea.Lihat Mentor Profile dan pilih dua mentor yang keahliannya cocok dengan ide Anda.
    3. Open Submit New Idea and fill in the five sections: Team, Your Idea, Business Impact, Support Needed and Preferred Mentors. Fields marked * are needed to submit.Buka Submit New Idea dan isi lima bagian: Team, Your Idea, Business Impact, Support Needed, dan Preferred Mentors. Kolom bertanda * wajib diisi untuk submit.
    4. Click Save draft at any time. Continue later from My Ideas with Continue editing.Klik Save draft kapan saja. Lanjutkan nanti dari My Ideas dengan Continue editing.
    5. When everything is ready, click Submit idea and confirm. If a field needs fixing, a summary tells you which ones.Jika semua sudah siap, klik Submit idea lalu konfirmasi. Jika ada kolom yang perlu diperbaiki, ringkasan akan menunjukkan kolom mana saja.

    You seeYang Anda lihat

    "Idea submitted", then the idea on My Ideas as Submitted. AIdea sends it to your Priority 1 mentor, or Priority 2 if the first is full."Idea submitted", lalu ide berstatus Submitted di My Ideas. AIdea mengirimnya ke mentor Priority 1, atau Priority 2 jika yang pertama penuh.

    Watch outPerhatikan

    • Submitting cannot be undone. Only an admin can change the team afterwards.Submit tidak bisa dibatalkan. Setelah itu hanya admin yang bisa mengubah tim.
    • Only the person who created a draft can continue editing it.Hanya pembuat draf yang bisa melanjutkan pengeditannya.
    • Submit before the closing date shown on Overview.Kirim sebelum tanggal penutupan yang tertera di Overview.

Common questionsPertanyaan umum

I can't sign in. What should I check?Saya tidak bisa login. Apa yang perlu dicek?

Use the same email or network ID and password you use for Windows. "Your Windows account is locked" or "Your Windows password has expired" means Godrej IT needs to help, or change your password on a company PC first. After 5 failed attempts, wait a few minutes before trying again.Gunakan email atau network ID dan password yang sama dengan Windows. Pesan "Your Windows account is locked" atau "Your Windows password has expired" berarti Anda perlu bantuan Godrej IT, atau ganti password dulu di PC kantor. Setelah 5 kali gagal, tunggu beberapa menit sebelum mencoba lagi.

Why can't I find a teammate in the search?Kenapa anggota tim tidak ditemukan di pencarian?

They need to sign in to AIdea once so their account exists. Type at least two letters of their name or email. A greyed-out name means that person has already committed to another idea.Mereka perlu login ke AIdea sekali agar akunnya tersedia. Ketik minimal dua huruf nama atau email. Nama berwarna abu-abu berarti orang tersebut sudah berkomitmen ke ide lain.

Can I change my idea after submitting it?Bisakah ide diubah setelah dikirim?

No. A submitted idea is locked. If the team needs to change, ask the program admin, who can update team membership.Tidak. Ide yang sudah dikirim terkunci. Jika tim perlu diubah, minta bantuan admin program, yang bisa memperbarui anggota tim.

Can I be on more than one idea?Bolehkah saya ikut di lebih dari satu ide?

Yes, until screening results are published. If more than one of your ideas passes, you choose one to commit to and leave the others.Boleh, sampai hasil screening dipublikasikan. Jika lebih dari satu ide Anda lolos, Anda memilih satu dan keluar dari ide lainnya.

My idea still shows N/A. Has something gone wrong?Ide saya masih N/A. Apakah ada masalah?

Usually not. Results for each stage appear only after the program team publishes them for all ideas. Not pass and Not build don't send a notification, so check My Ideas after the dates on Overview.Biasanya tidak. Hasil setiap tahap baru muncul setelah tim program mempublikasikannya untuk semua ide. Not pass dan Not build tidak mengirim notifikasi, jadi cek My Ideas setelah tanggal di Overview.

Can I see my score or the mentor's comments?Bisakah saya melihat nilai atau komentar mentor?

No. Participants see outcomes only. Your project mentor is the best person to talk to about how to strengthen the idea.Tidak. Peserta hanya melihat hasil akhir. Mentor proyek adalah orang yang tepat untuk diskusi cara memperkuat ide Anda.

Why can't I vote for my own idea, or why is Voting empty?Kenapa saya tidak bisa memilih ide sendiri, atau kenapa Voting kosong?

No one can vote for a team they lead or belong to. The ballot appears only while voting is open; outside that window you see "Voting isn't open right now".Tidak ada yang bisa memilih tim yang dipimpin atau diikutinya. Daftar voting hanya muncul saat voting dibuka; di luar waktu itu tampil "Voting isn't open right now".

What is "Acting as" in the header?Apa itu "Acting as" di bagian atas?

Every account is also an Employee Voter. Switching to Employee Voter shows only Voting, Mentor Profile and Notifications. Switch back to Participant to see My Ideas and Submit New Idea.Setiap akun juga merupakan Employee Voter. Jika beralih ke Employee Voter, hanya Voting, Mentor Profile, dan Notifications yang tampil. Kembali ke Participant untuk melihat My Ideas dan Submit New Idea.
